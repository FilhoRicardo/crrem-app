# European Portfolio

EU-only sub-portfolio (30,000 m² GIA, London + Berlin). Both assets have HDD/CDD climate adjustment switched on (Northgate RCP 4.5, Lichtenberg RCP 8.5), making this the right portfolio to demonstrate the climate-scenario feature in the rollup view.

Useful for sustainability reporting under EU Taxonomy / SFDR — UK + Germany jurisdictions both have well-defined CRREM pathways and bundled HDD/CDD curves.
